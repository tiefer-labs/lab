# Code of conduct

Status: in use. Owner: Tiefer. Licence: MPL 2.0.

How people are expected to behave in every space of the `tiefer-labs` organisation, how to report a problem, and how reports are handled. This is the Lab copy of the organisation's code of conduct; its substance is the same.

---

## 1. Purpose

Tiefer's repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions are professional, technical spaces. People take part from different countries, languages, backgrounds and levels of experience. This code exists so that everyone can do careful work here without being harassed, excluded or misled.

---

## 2. Scope

This code applies:

- in every repository of the `tiefer-labs` organisation on GitHub, including issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review comments, discussions and wikis;
- in any other channel run by Tiefer, such as mailing lists, chat groups and events;
- outside these spaces when a person represents Tiefer, for example when presenting Tiefer's work, or when behaviour outside these spaces affects the safety of people inside them.

It applies to everyone: maintainers, contributors, users, customers and visitors.

---

## 3. Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Criticise ideas, code and results, never people.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept feedback in the same way.
- Keep discussions on topic. Open a new issue for a new subject.
- Be honest about evidence. State what was measured, how and where, and say plainly when something was not measured.
- Credit other people's work, data and ideas, and respect their licences.
- Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, location or images without their permission.
- Write so that people who are not native English speakers can follow: short sentences, defined terms, no unexplained jargon.
- Remember that maintainers are a small team with limited time.

---

## 4. Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or content based on any personal characteristic, including age, body, disability, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, language or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, spam or repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), including information that could locate or identify a person from satellite or other imagery.
- Knowingly presenting fabricated, manipulated or unsourced results as measured, or misrepresenting someone else's results.
- Submitting code, data or text that you do not have the right to contribute.
- Encouraging, or advocating for, any of the above.
- Retaliating against anyone who reports a problem in good faith.

---

## 5. Reporting

Write to [hello@tiefer.space](mailto:hello@tiefer.space) with the subject line "Code of conduct".

Please include, as far as you can:

| Information | Example |
| :--- | :---: |
| What happened | a description in your own words |
| Where | a link to the issue, pull request, comment or channel |
| When | date and time, with time zone |
| Who was involved | GitHub usernames or names, if known |
| Evidence | screenshots or copies, since comments can be edited or deleted |
| Whether it is ongoing | yes or no |
| What you would like to happen | optional |

Reports are handled confidentially. The identity of the person reporting is shared only with the people handling the report, and only with their permission is it shared with anyone else.

If the report concerns a person who would normally handle it, say so in the report. That person is excluded from handling it.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first.

---

## 6. How reports are handled

| Step | Target time |
| :--- | :---: |
| Acknowledge the report | within 3 working days |
| Review the facts and, where needed, hear the people involved | within 14 days of the report |
| Decide on an action and tell the person who reported it | at the end of the review |

Working days are Monday to Friday in Baku, Azerbaijan (UTC+4), excluding public holidays. Every report is reviewed, including anonymous ones, although an anonymous report may limit what can be checked.

---

## 7. Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests and other contributions that break this code, and may lock conversations. Actions follow the impact of the behaviour; the steps below are a guide, not a fixed sequence.

| Step | When it is used | Consequence |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 1. Correction | inappropriate language or other behaviour seen as unprofessional | a private written note explaining what was wrong; a public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | a single serious incident or a series of minor ones | a written warning; no interaction with the people involved for a stated period |
| 3. Temporary ban | a serious incident or sustained inappropriate behaviour | no interaction of any kind with Tiefer's spaces for a stated period |
| 4. Permanent ban | a pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression towards a group | permanent removal from all of Tiefer's spaces |

---

## 8. Appeals

A person who is subject to an action may appeal once, within 30 days, by writing to [hello@tiefer.space](mailto:hello@tiefer.space) with the subject line "Code of conduct appeal". Where possible, the appeal is reviewed by someone who did not take the original decision.
