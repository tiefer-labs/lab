| Mark | Where it appears |
| :--- | :---: |
| The word "Tiefer" | the name of the company and of its software, for example "Tiefer Lab" |

## 2. Allowed without permission

- Referring to Tiefer or its software truthfully, for example "this project uses code from Tiefer Lab".
- Linking to Tiefer's repositories and website.
- Saying in plain text that a fork or a derived work is "based on Tiefer Lab".
- Quoting a result of Tiefer Lab with its source, as set out in [BENCHMARK-AUTHORITY.md](BENCHMARK-AUTHORITY.md), section 4.

---

## 3. Not allowed without written permission

- Using the name or the logo in the name of a product, a fork, a service, a company or a domain.
- Changing, recolouring or combining the logo with other marks.
- Implying that Tiefer endorses, certifies or partners with a person, product or result when it does not.
- Using the marks in any way that may confuse users about who made a product or who is responsible for it.

---

## 4. Forks

A fork keeps the MPL 2.0 licence of the files it copies, but not the marks. Before publishing a fork:

1. Remove `docs/assets/header.png`, `docs/assets/tiefer-logo.svg` and `docs/assets/tiefer-logo-white.svg`, and the image line at the top of each Markdown file.
2. Rename the project so that its name does not contain "Tiefer".
3. Keep the licence notices and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) as MPL 2.0 requires.

To ask for permission, or to report a use of the marks that you believe breaks this policy, write to [hello@tiefer.space](mailto:hello@tiefer.space) with the subject line "Trademark".
